## One decision we should make before costing the facility

There is a meaningful difference between renting an ordinary office and renting a space suitable for a small GPU research facility. I would search for the latter.

The next useful step is to establish the intended facility scale. **Are you picturing a dedicated small commercial/industrial unit for the workstations and development area, or a conventional office suite with a separate equipment room?**

That distinction will affect the electrical, cooling, rental, and build-out estimates considerably.
